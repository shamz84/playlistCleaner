8TV Player · interactive mock-up

More room for your Live TV guide

Compare three fictional layouts. Each uses the requested flow: Back from full-screen → guide with preview → categories slide out. Nothing here changes the app or plays a stream.

Use the buttons, or focus a control and use arrow keys and Enter. Esc / Backspace simulates the remote Back button; the preview is a placeholder.

A · Balanced

Programme details and preview stay prominent, with a wider guide when categories are closed.

B · More guide rows

A shorter information area and denser rows show more channels at once.

C · Wider channel names

A wider channel column and taller rows favour legibility over the number of visible listings.

Picture-format settings in this demo are independent and temporary. Normal (fit / letterbox), Full (stretch), 4:3, 16:9, 2.35:1 and Zoom are illustrative options; changing them here does not affect a real video.

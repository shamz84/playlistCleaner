8TV Player · interactive design demo

Choosing seasons on TV

Try three directions for a show with many seasons. This is a mock-up using fictional content; it does not play video or change your app.

Click a control to start. Inside the TV preview, use arrow keys to move and Enter to select; Esc closes the versions panel. Mouse and touch also work.

A · Seasons first

Season tabs lead directly to episodes. Versions and filters stay tucked away until needed.

B · Side-by-side

A season list stays beside the episodes. Useful for navigating a show with many seasons.

C · Compact selector

Previous/next controls keep the season selector compact, with an optional full season picker.

Selection behaviour in this demo: seasons are never hidden by a quality or language choice. The preferred source is used where available; an episode can fall back to another source. Availability and labels are illustrative.

Live TV layout options (left-hand side)

Static mock-ups, not the app. Goal: no wasted space on the left, TiviMate-style. In every option the playlists can be shown in the order you choose (reorderable in Settings, and Move up/down on the playlist header in Live TV). Option A is closest to your last TiviMate photo.

Thin icon rail, playlist + group tree, then preview/details and guide. Everything visible at once; the tree scrolls.
